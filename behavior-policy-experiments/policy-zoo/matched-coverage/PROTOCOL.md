# Same coverage, different movement: fixed comparison

Specified before the main fits. This is an exploratory mechanism study motivated by the existing zoo, not a fresh algorithm ranking.

- Unchanged observations: triangle vertices divided by distance. One physical coordinate, distances 1–16, actions approach/stay/retreat by 0.25. Collection starts on a fixed 61-state reachable lattice; this replaces the old continuously sampled resets and is stated explicitly. No extra coordinates or action magnitudes.
- Shared stationary marginal: a finite log-uniform approximation, integrated over physical Voronoi cells and rounded to 65,536 integer counts. Both ends of every dataset have exactly these counts. Every policy covers all 61 states with identical bin counts, entropy and distance distribution. The common target is fixed before fitting and is not the zoo's old on-policy occupancy.
- Four fixed policies: reference local walk; reduced crossing near distance 2.5; reduced crossing near 8; ICM-shaped crossing strengths derived from the existing frozen seed-13 ICM actor. The last is a new symmetrized, coverage-corrected policy, not a rerun of original ICM. Actor seed 13 is fixed for all training seeds. No claim of generalization across independently learned ICM actors.
- Integer symmetric edge flows establish detailed balance exactly. Total edge flow is identical across policies: approach/stay/retreat frequencies, mean absolute physical step, and mean squared physical step all match. Conditional action probabilities and crossing locations differ. Their log-distance step moments need not match.
- Stationary stratified one-step datasets enumerate these flows; they are not single trajectories. Thus finite coverage matches exactly without relying on burn-in or importance weights. The policies are time independent and have the stated stationary distribution. Actual rollout mixing times may differ; those collection-efficiency effects are deliberately excluded here. Minibatch marginal fluctuations need not match on both ends, despite identical full-dataset marginals.
- Keep the existing encoder, SIGReg, 0.1 regularization weight, 512-pair batches, Adam and 4,000-update schedule. No handover, geometry penalty, action input to encoder, or regularizer ablation. Seeds 100–109 pair initialization and minibatch indices across all four conditions. Seeds vary training randomness and within-state pair ordering, not the exact transition law or ICM actor.
- Identifiability diagnostics: primary stationary-weighted affine log-distance R²; also uniform-log-grid R², physical-distance R² and reversed dense-grid fraction. Good R² is recovery evidence, not a theorem. A fold flag means any reversed dense-grid segment at the pre-existing 1e-6 threshold.
- Planning: freeze encoder; fit the existing action-conditioned predictor for 2,000 updates on 65,536 observation/action transitions. Shared-data arm uses identical feasible-action data for every encoder; native-data arm uses each policy's matched-occupancy data. Action frequencies of native sets match globally, but action coverage at particular distances can differ. Both arms use identical initialization, predictor minibatch indices, calibration data and reachable goals.
- 64 fixed full-range goals per seed, 12 replanning actions, horizon 8, 128 candidate sequences including every reachable net displacement. Score actual terminal physical-distance error, success within 0.25, and near/middle/far errors. An explicit physical oracle verifies zero error on every case. The planner receives only embeddings and action-conditioned predictions. Region boundaries are fixed at starting distance 4 and 9. Predictor loss is secondary to actual reaching.
- Primary paired tests: three policies versus reference, for log-distance R² and shared-data goal error: six exact two-sided sign tests with one Holm correction. Native-data errors are a separate secondary family of three. All seeds retained; no success-conditioned subsets. Two-sided sign tests concern seed-wise win probability; unadjusted bootstrap intervals concern mean differences. Goal cases are not independent experimental replicates.
- Slow features: exact eigendecomposition of each reversible finite transition matrix. Exclude the constant eigenvector. The first three nonconstant modes, eigenvalues and alignment energies are saved. These characterize the whitening-constrained alignment objective, not the exact minimizer of alignment plus finite SIGReg.
- No policies selected or parameters tuned using the new outcomes. Run files record a code/settings hash. Encoder/optimizer checkpoints every 1,000 updates allow restart; completed planning arms are deterministically reproducible, though an interrupted arm restarts its predictor fit.
