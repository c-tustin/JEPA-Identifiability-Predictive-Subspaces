# How the two new-policy studies are compared

The Gaussian-targeted study fixes its candidate settings and confirms four discovery-selected combinations on seeds 28–47. The broader-coverage follow-up is informed by the earlier discovery failures, retains its own fixed settings, and confirms four combinations on disjoint seeds 48–67. The four candidates within each block are selected using only seeds 8–27, by the rule in that study's protocol.

The overview will additionally apply **one Holm correction across all eight fresh-seed contrasts** against OU, and separately across all eight contrasts against the best old policy and against random. This is a conservative campaign-wide summary, added before the broader study's confirmation. It does not loosen or replace either original four-test family, and it does not pool old discovery seeds into confirmation.

Call a consistent improvement only when the observed mean difference is positive, wins exceed losses, and this combined adjusted two-sided sign-test p is below 0.05. The sign test concerns non-tie win probability, not expected mean improvement. Paired-bootstrap mean intervals are unadjusted summaries. Differences between third-decimal representation scores need not imply better planning.

Always report failed candidates, fold counts, the old best-policy comparison, and each study's seed block. A win against OU alone is not a claim to beat the strongest existing zoo policy.
