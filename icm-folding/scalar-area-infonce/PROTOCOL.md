# Scalar projected-area InfoNCE protocol

Fixed November 19, 2026 before training. Replace each six-coordinate observation by the unsigned area of its three projected vertices, computed by the shoelace/determinant formula. For the fixed frontoparallel triangle scaled by inverse distance, area is A0/d², strictly decreasing for positive distance. Coordinates are used only in the observation adapter; the learned models receive one scalar. The actor and ICM were originally trained with coordinates and remain frozen. These are reused trajectories, not scalar-observation actor retraining or new source collection. Shape is fixed in this experiment; varying shape independently can make area ambiguous about distance.

Use seeds 130–144 and the existing episode split and transition index plan. Train from scratch for 4,000 Adam updates, learning rate .001 for half the budget then cosine decay. Positive objects are scalar area and its own code; each batch contains 128 current plus 128 successor areas and 255 negatives per code. Temporal correspondence and actions do not enter the encoder loss. Duplicate objects remain negatives.

Encoder: input tanh(4A), then Linear(1,48), Tanh, Linear(48,48), Tanh, Linear(48,1). Critic: Linear(1,48), Tanh, Linear(48,48), Tanh, Linear(48,1). Area candidates use training-endpoint mean and population standard deviation, floored at 1e-6. Score is -(g(z)-standardized_area)²/.2. Optimize encoder and critic jointly with InfoNCE cross-entropy only. No EMA, alignment, geometry labels, order constraint, extra reconstruction loss or hyperparameter selection. The changed input/output dimensions mean initialization is deterministic but not identical to the old networks. Adam batch indices and source datasets match the six-coordinate raw/code run.

Record fixed train/held-out 256-object InfoNCE evaluations and representation snapshots every 100 updates, including initialization. Independently reload final encoders for 20,001-point float64 derivative checks on physical distance [1,16]. These finite-grid checks are empirical, not an injectivity guarantee. Report output variation, turns, and affine log-distance R². Physical distance enters simulation and evaluation only.

Freeze each final scalar encoder. Fit a separate 4→64→64→1 ELU decoder from normalized scalar code and one-hot action to next scalar area, whitened with training-successor area statistics (population std floor 1e-6). Use 1,500 Adam .001 updates, batch 256, deterministic seed/index stream. Evaluate training/held-out and uniform-distance coverage MSE plus an action-only baseline. These targets differ from the six-coordinate study, so their MSEs are not directly comparable. Record whole-dataset decoder errors every 100 decoder updates.

Retain source/adapter/code/protocol hashes, initial and final weights, optimizer state, fixed statistics, histories and decoder states. Verify area by independent shoelace/known-triangle calculations, translation and rotation invariance and quadratic scaling; verify scalar tensor shapes, raw detachment, explicit-loop scores, gradients, paired sampling and independent checkpoint geometry/decoder reloads. No new actor performance claim or robustness to variable shape/noise is implied.
