# Occupancy and folding: exploratory intervention

Fixed before new results, 2026-11-14. Reuse the established gain-4 scalar encoder, 4,000 updates, 512 alignment pairs, 1,024 regularizer endpoints, weight 0.1, cosine schedule, and stored pure-ICM transitions. No OU phase. Physical distance is used to construct controlled sampling and evaluate, never as an encoder label.

1. Native occupancy: seeds 130–139; compare ordinary SIGReg, inverse-occupancy scaling of its output gradients with ordinary characteristic-function moments, and inverse-occupancy-weighted characteristic-function moments. Only SIGReg changes. Use 32 equal log-distance bins and the full stored endpoint histogram; normalize weights in each batch. Empty bins cannot be repaired.
2. Controlled occupancy: diagnostic seeds 130, 131, 136. Keep each seed's alignment data unchanged. Independently sample 1,024 regularizer observations from a common full-support pool on log-distance [0, log 16]. Compare a peak at d=2; two peaks at 2 and 8; three peaks at 2, 5 and 12; and uniform log-distance. Each mixture has 10% uniform background, equal peak masses, and log-width 0.14. Define mass on 64 log-distance bins and sample uniformly inside each bin. The three gradient/moment methods use identical sample draws; uniform needs only the ordinary condition. Inverse mass targets uniform log-distance, not uniform physical distance. This is an occupancy-only regularizer intervention, not a newly trained bimodal/trimodal ICM policy.
3. Store raw curves and analytic slopes every 25 updates. Locate every sign-changing slope root on a 1,025-point log grid; retain roots entering or leaving [1,16]. Report final roots and reversal fraction, and log-distance affine R². A root is a descriptive finite-grid folding diagnostic, not a certificate of injectivity.
4. Inspect all ten saved actors and curiosity predictors: action probabilities, reflected expected distance drift, intrinsic reward by action, occupancy, and a frozen-policy Markov-chain comparison. Trace policy training on three diagnostic seeds if needed. These distinguish state attraction from encoder folding. Current frozen reward maps alone cannot prove the historical cause of policy learning.

The gradient-only intervention is deliberately distinct from importance-weighting SIGReg's empirical moments. It changes a vector field and need not be the gradient of any scalar objective. Neither method guarantees monotonicity. These seeds have been inspected before; no independent confirmatory claim or p-value will be made.
