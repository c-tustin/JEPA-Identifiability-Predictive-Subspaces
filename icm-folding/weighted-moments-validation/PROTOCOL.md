2. For every gain-4 ordinary fit ending folded on the dense grid, continue its exact encoder for 8,000 updates at constant Adam rate 0.0001. Never count reinitializing an encoder as repair. Report every eligible fold; if none, explicitly report no new direct-repair test.
3. No order prior, collision/mining gradient, encoder-class change, actor update, hyperparameter sweep or outcome-dependent exclusions. Gain 8 is a prespecified stress condition known to fail in historical runs; do not pool it with gain 4 to hide differences.

Report any derivative reversal, material backward variation (>0.001 of total variation), output standard deviation, and affine log-distance R². Retain histories, parameters, Adam state, data/source/code hashes and paired plan hashes. Nonconstant geometry is required for interpreting an ordered curve as useful; no downstream-performance claim is made.

Historical evidence is reported separately: gain-4 weighted moments 0/10 final folds from initialization; gain-8 7/10 final folds; all five historical late interventions remain folded whether Adam is retained or reset. The new results can support or contradict prevention in the specified configuration, but cannot establish universal injectivity or an asymptotic success rate.
